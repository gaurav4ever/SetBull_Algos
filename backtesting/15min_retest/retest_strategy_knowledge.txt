Phase 4: 5MR Retest Strategy - Detailed Understanding
Trade Management System
Phase 4 implements a comprehensive trade management system that handles a trade from entry to exit, maximizing profits while managing risk. This phase is divided into three main components:
1. Active Trade Management
Once a trade is entered, the system actively manages its lifecycle through several key mechanisms:
Stop Loss Management:

Initial stop loss is placed at a fixed percentage (0.75%) from entry price
When price reaches 1:1 risk-reward ratio, stop loss moves to breakeven
After TP2 is hit, stop loss trails to lock in partial profits

Take Profit System:

Implements a three-tiered scaling out approach
TP1: Takes 10% of position off at 1:3 risk-reward ratio
TP2: Takes 40% of position off at 1:5 risk-reward ratio
TP3: Takes remaining 50% off at 1:7 risk-reward ratio
Each exit calculates and tracks its contribution to total PnL

Visual Indicators:

Marks significant events during the trade (SL, BE, TP1, TP2, TP3)
Updates trade-related visualizations throughout the trade's lifecycle
Shows the progression of the trade management process

2. End of Day Processing
The strategy includes safeguards for trades that remain open at the end of the trading day:
Position Closing:

Automatically closes any open positions at the end of the trading day (3:15-3:20 PM)
Calculates partial P&L for trades with partial exits already taken
Adds the end-of-day exit P&L to the total trade performance

P&L Accounting:

Combines P&L from partial exits with the final exit for accurate tracking
Determines whether the day resulted in a win or loss
Records the day's performance metrics for statistical tracking

3. Performance Analysis
The final component provides detailed statistical analysis of strategy performance:
Comprehensive Metrics:

Trade frequency: Total trading days vs. days with actual trades
Win/loss ratios: Overall and broken down by direction (long/short)
P&L metrics: Average win/loss, largest win/loss, net P&L
Consistency metrics: Max consecutive wins/losses, win streaks

Performance Visualization:

Creates a detailed results table with color-coded performance metrics
Automatically calculates derivative metrics like profit factor and expectancy
Provides directional analysis to identify strategy biases
Shows risk-adjusted performance statistics

Market Psychology Insights:

Tracks streaks to identify potential psychological impacts
Separates long and short performance to reveal market biases
Provides context for understanding when the strategy performs best/worst
